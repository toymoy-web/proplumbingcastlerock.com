What to Do Right Now

  • Shut off the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the home.
  • Turn off electricity to the affected area if water is near outlets or a panel.
  • Move valuables and furniture away from the leak if it is safe to do so.
  • Photograph the damage for insurance before cleanup begins.
  • Do not use electrical appliances in standing water.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line rather than waiting for a scheduled appointment.
Local Cause

Why This Happens More in Castle Rock Winters

At 6,202 feet, Castle Rock sees harder, longer overnight freezes than lower parts of the Denver metro. Pipes running through garages, crawlspaces, and exterior walls without adequate insulation are the most common failure points, especially during a hard cold snap after a warm stretch, when a homeowner has not yet thought to check exposed lines.

Newer construction is not automatically safer either. A garage line run close to an exterior wall in a home built in the last few years can still freeze if insulation was installed to code minimums rather than for this elevation's actual overnight lows. Every repair we make still follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by Colorado, regardless of the home's age.

Vacant or lightly heated homes are at particular risk during a hard freeze. A thermostat set too low while a family is traveling can let interior temperatures drop enough for even well-insulated interior walls to see a pipe failure, especially on the north side of the house.

If you are planning to travel during winter, leaving the thermostat no lower than the mid-fifties and shutting off the main valve for an extended vacancy are both worth doing before you leave.

Prevention

Reducing Freeze Risk Next Winter

Insulate Exposed Pipes

Garage and crawlspace lines benefit most from foam sleeve insulation.

Seal Drafts

Gaps near pipe penetrations let cold air reach lines that would otherwise stay protected.

Know Your Shutoff

Every household should know where the main valve is before an emergency happens.

Keep a Trickle Running

A slow drip during extreme cold snaps can relieve pressure in vulnerable lines.
