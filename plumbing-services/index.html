How We Price Every Job

The Same Process, Every Time

Regardless of which service you need, the process is the same: we inspect the actual issue, confirm a flat price before any work begins, and complete the job or explain honestly if it needs a follow-up visit. Free estimates apply to non-emergency work; emergency dispatch is quoted on-site before the repair starts.

Every technician is Colorado DORA-licensed, and every job follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by the state, regardless of which service page brought you here.

Emergency vs. Scheduled Work

Knowing Which You Need

Burst pipes, active leaks flooding a room, sewage backups, and total loss of water or hot water all qualify for our 24/7 emergency line. A dripping faucet, a toilet that runs occasionally, or a planned upgrade like a water softener install can typically be scheduled for a convenient time instead.

Planned Projects

Bigger Jobs Worth Planning Ahead

Whole-home repiping, a basement finish's plumbing rough-in, or a full water heater replacement are all worth scheduling in advance rather than waiting for an emergency to force the decision. We can walk through timeline and cost expectations for any of these during an initial call, before committing to anything.

If you are unsure whether your situation is an emergency or something that can wait, call and describe it. We would rather make that call together than have you guess wrong in either direction.

Serving Both Residential and Commercial

Homes and Businesses

While most of our calls come from homeowners, our commercial plumbing service covers property managers, restaurants, and retail spaces throughout our coverage area. The same upfront pricing and diagnostic-first approach applies regardless of whether the address is residential or commercial.

If you manage multiple properties and want a standing relationship rather than starting from scratch on every call, mention that when you reach out. We can talk through what a more coordinated arrangement would look like.
