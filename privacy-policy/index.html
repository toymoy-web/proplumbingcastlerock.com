Privacy Policy

How Castle Rock Pro Plumbing collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

When you call, email, or submit information to Castle Rock Pro Plumbing, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you are contacting us about. We use this information solely to schedule and provide the service you requested.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to service requests, schedule appointments, provide quotes, and communicate with you about ongoing or completed work. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

We may share your information with subcontractors or partners directly involved in completing your requested service, such as a specialist we coordinate with for a specific job. We do not share your information for marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide to us. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to any recordkeeping requirements tied to completed work.

Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to contact@proplumbingcastlerock.com or (303) 552-3896.
