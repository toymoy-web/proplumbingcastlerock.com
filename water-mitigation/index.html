What to Do in the First Hour

  • Stop the water source if it is safe to do so, such as shutting off the main valve.
  • Turn off electricity to affected areas before entering standing water.
  • Remove or elevate rugs, furniture legs, and electronics from the wet area.
  • Photograph the damage before any items are moved, for your insurance claim.
  • Avoid using a household fan in a way that spreads water to dry areas.
  • Call our water damage line rather than waiting to see how bad it gets.
Mitigation vs. Restoration

Two Different Stages

Mitigation is the immediate response: stopping the water, extracting what has pooled, and starting the drying process within the first hours of an event. Restoration is the fuller process that follows, addressing anything mitigation alone could not fully dry or save. Acting fast at the mitigation stage is what keeps a job small instead of letting it become a full restoration project.

In practice, we often start mitigation and continue straight into restoration on the same visit if the damage calls for it, but they are billed and scoped as the distinct stages they are, not bundled into one vague line item.

Castle Rock's basement-heavy housing stock means mitigation calls here skew toward finished living spaces rather than unfinished utility areas, which raises the stakes on how quickly we get equipment on-site once you call.

Air movers and dehumidifiers placed within the first few hours can often save carpet and drywall that would otherwise need full replacement if the same water sat untreated overnight.

We track drying progress with moisture readings rather than a visual check alone, since materials can look dry on the surface while still holding enough moisture underneath to grow mold within days.

We do not close out a mitigation job on a visual estimate. Documented moisture readings at the start and end of the job also give you a clear record for insurance if the claim is questioned later.

If mold has already started to grow before we arrive, we will tell you honestly rather than proceeding with standard drying alone, since visible mold growth calls for a different scope of work than routine extraction and drying.

Every technician we send carries moisture meters and thermal cameras as standard equipment, not optional add-ons, so the same crew that responds to your call can also confirm when the job is actually finished.
