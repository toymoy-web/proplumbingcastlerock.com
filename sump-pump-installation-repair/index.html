Signs You Need Service

Warning Signs Before a Failure

Pump Runs Constantly

A pump that never seems to shut off may be undersized or losing efficiency.

Unusual Noises

Grinding or rattling often means a failing motor or a damaged impeller.

Musty Basement Smell

Can indicate the pump is not keeping up with normal groundwater seepage.

Visible Age or Rust

Most pumps last 7 to 10 years; older units are worth testing before the next storm.

Local Cause

Why This Matters in Castle Rock

Finished basements are common across Castle Rock neighborhoods from The Meadows to Founders Village, which means a sump pump failure does not just mean a wet crawlspace, it can mean real damage to a finished living space. Spring snowmelt and summer storm runoff are the two periods we see the most sump-related calls each year.

A pump that has never failed does not mean it is fine indefinitely. Most failures happen without warning during the exact storm that puts the most demand on the system, which is why a seasonal test matters more than waiting for a visible problem.

A discharge line that empties too close to the foundation can also undo the pump's work by letting water seep right back toward the basement, so we check the discharge routing as part of any install or repair, not just the pump itself.

We also check the check valve on the discharge line during any service call, since a failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit after each cycle, making the pump work harder and shortening its life for a problem that is simple to fix.

A pit liner cracked from age can also let soil and debris into the system, which is a common cause of a pump jamming even when the motor itself is otherwise healthy. Every install follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by Colorado.

Backup Options

Primary Pump vs. Battery Backup

Primary Pump Only

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Handles normal seepage and rainfall
  • No protection during a power outage
  • Common baseline setup

Primary + Battery Backup

  • Keeps working during a power outage
  • Protects against pump motor failure
  • Higher upfront cost
  • Recommended for finished basements
