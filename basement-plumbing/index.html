Local Cause

Why This Matters in Castle Rock

Finished basements are common across Castle Rock neighborhoods from The Meadows to Founders Village, and because most fixtures below the main sewer line's grade need an ejector pump to lift waste up to that line, basement bathroom additions here are rarely a simple drain tie-in. We plan the ejector system as part of the project from the start, not as an afterthought.

A basement finish project without a plumber involved from the planning stage often runs into surprises once drywall is up, whether that is an ejector pump sized wrong for the fixture count or a floor drain that was never tied properly into the main line. Getting the plumbing scope right before framing starts avoids reopening finished walls later.

Utility sinks and wet bars are another common basement addition that people underestimate, since even a simple sink drain below grade usually needs the same ejector consideration as a full bathroom, just on a smaller scale.

We also see homeowners assume a basement egress window well drain is a plumbing fixture when it is not, a distinction worth clarifying early since egress drainage typically falls under a different scope of work entirely.

If your basement finish plan is still in the early stages, we are happy to walk the space with you before any contracts are signed, so the plumbing budget is realistic from the very first conversation rather than a surprise partway through.

That early walkthrough costs you nothing and often saves real money later, since fixture placement decided before framing is far cheaper to adjust than after walls and floors are already finished. Every rough-in follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by Colorado.

We are also glad to answer a quick question over the phone before you even schedule anything, if you are still deciding whether a full plumbing consultation makes sense for where your project currently stands.
