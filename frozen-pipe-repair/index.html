What to Do If You Suspect a Frozen Pipe

  • Shut off the main water valve if you suspect the pipe has already split.
  • Open the nearest faucet slightly to relieve pressure in the line, if it is not already leaking.
  • Never use an open flame, blowtorch, or unattended space heater to thaw a pipe yourself.
  • Keep the area near the suspected freeze warm if possible, using safe methods like a hair dryer at a distance.
  • Photograph any resulting damage for insurance once water starts flowing again.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line rather than waiting to see if it thaws safely on its own.
Local Cause

Why This Happens at Castle Rock's Elevation

At 6,202 feet, Castle Rock sees harder, longer overnight freezes than lower parts of the Denver metro. Pipes in garages, crawlspaces, and exterior walls without adequate insulation are the most likely to freeze, especially during a sudden cold snap after a milder stretch of weather.

Homes with additions or bonus rooms built over an unheated garage are a particular risk area, since supply lines run through that transition zone often see less consistent heat than the rest of the house.

A single hard freeze does not usually cause a burst on its own. It is typically a multi-day cold snap, especially one following a warmer stretch that let a homeowner's guard down on space heating in vulnerable areas.

We see a real spike in these calls during the first hard freeze of the season each year, often right after an unusually mild start to winter, when insulation gaps that were never a problem before suddenly matter. Every repair follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by Colorado.

Prevention

Reducing Freeze Risk

Insulate Exposed Lines

Foam pipe sleeves in garages and crawlspaces make a real difference.

Seal Air Leaks

Gaps near pipe penetrations let cold air reach otherwise protected lines.

Keep Interior Doors Open

Let warm air reach cabinets housing pipes under sinks.

Maintain Heat While Away

Keep the thermostat no lower than the mid-fifties during winter travel.
