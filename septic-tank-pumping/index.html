Signs You Need Pumping

Common Warning Signs

Slow Drains Throughout

Every fixture draining slowly at once, not just one, points to the tank or field.

Sewage Odor Outdoors

A smell near the tank or drain field area is a clear warning sign.

Soggy Ground Over the Field

Standing water or unusually lush grass over the drain field can mean overflow.

Gurgling Drains

Air trapped by a full tank can gurgle back up through household drains.

Local Cause

A Note on Castle Rock's Sewer Coverage

Most addresses within Town of Castle Rock limits connect to municipal sewer through Castle Rock Water, but properties in unincorporated pockets of Douglas County, and some larger-lot properties on the town's edges, still rely on a septic system. If you are unsure which applies to your property, we can help confirm during a service call.

Even on septic, wastewater habits from a municipal-sewer mindset can shorten a tank's service life. Garbage disposal use, harsh chemical drain cleaners, and excessive water softener discharge all put more strain on a septic system's bacterial balance than they would on a municipal treatment plant built to handle them at scale.

A drain field that has been driven or parked on can also compact the soil enough to reduce its absorption capacity over time, a preventable issue that has nothing to do with the tank itself but still shows up as the same slow-drain symptoms homeowners call about.

Keeping a simple record of pumping dates also helps future service calls go faster, since knowing the last pump date lets us judge whether a current issue is overdue maintenance or something more specific to address.

If you recently purchased a property and are not sure of its septic history, we can start that record for you at the first pumping, giving you a documented baseline instead of an unknown.

That first visit is also a good time to ask questions about your specific system, since septic setups vary enough in age and design that general advice online rarely fits every property exactly.

We would rather answer those questions plainly during a routine pumping than have you piece together generic advice that may or may not apply to your actual tank and drain field configuration.

Before We Arrive

If You Have an Active Backup

  • Stop using all water in the house, including toilets, sinks, and washing machines.
  • Avoid walking through or touching standing wastewater directly.
  • Keep children and pets away from the affected area.
  • Ventilate the area if there is a strong odor, opening a window if possible.
  • Photograph any damage for insurance before cleanup begins.
  • Call us rather than attempting to access or open the tank yourself.
