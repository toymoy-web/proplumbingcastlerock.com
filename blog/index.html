About These Guides

Real Local Research, Not Generic Advice

Every guide here is written with Castle Rock's actual conditions in mind: our elevation and freeze pattern, the Pierre Shale and Denver Formation clay under much of town, our water hardness, and the mix of older and newer housing stock across our service area. We do not publish generic national advice with the city name swapped in.

Have a topic you would like covered that is not here yet? Call us and ask. We are always adding new guides based on the questions Castle Rock homeowners actually bring us.

More guides are added regularly, so check back or bookmark this page if you want to follow along.
