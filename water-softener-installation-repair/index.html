Why This Matters in Castle Rock Specifically

Castle Rock Water reports moderately hard to hard water in the 108 to 125 ppm range. That mineral content is what causes white spots on glassware, scale rings around faucets and shower heads, and a water heater or tankless unit that scales internally faster than it would with softer water.

A whole-home softener treats the water before it reaches fixtures and appliances, which is a different and generally more effective approach than treating symptoms one fixture at a time.

We also see this scale buildup show up in less obvious places, like a dishwasher that stops rinsing effectively or a coffee maker that needs frequent descaling long before the manual suggests it should.

Laundry is another area where hard water shows its cost, since detergent works less effectively in hard water and fabrics can feel stiffer over time, both signs that are easy to attribute to the wrong cause without knowing your actual water hardness.

We can test your actual tap water hardness on-site rather than relying only on the utility's published range, since plumbing age and any existing partial treatment can shift the real number a household experiences.

That on-site number is what actually determines the right system size and regeneration settings, not the general city-wide figure alone, which is why we always test before recommending a specific model.

Once a system is sized correctly, most households notice the difference within the first week, in softer-feeling water and less visible residue around fixtures.

We also check your existing supply line material before installation, since older galvanized plumbing can restrict flow in ways that affect how well any softener performs regardless of how well it is sized. All connections follow the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by Colorado.

Signs You Need Service

Repair or Replace

Salt Bridge in the Tank

A hardened salt crust can stop the regeneration cycle from working properly.

No Improvement in Water Feel

Water that still feels hard after installation often means a resin bed issue.

Unit Running Constantly

Excessive regeneration cycles can mean the system is sized wrong for the household.

Age Over 10-15 Years

Older units may be less efficient than a modern replacement, even if still working.
